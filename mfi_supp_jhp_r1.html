Item generation and preliminary psychometric evaluation of the Migraine Freedom Index: an exploratory study linked to a headache-diary application (n=115)

The questionnaire was developed and administered in Japanese. The English wording of the items is a provisional translation by the study team; a formal forward and back translation and cognitive debriefing have not been done. The items did not state a recall period. All items used a 7-point agreement scale (0 = strongly disagree, 6 = strongly agree) and were phrased positively, with no reverse-scored items. For Life Restoration, a higher score indicates that more of daily life is restored. For Migraine Agency, a higher score indicates more awareness and management of migraine and does not necessarily indicate a better state. Each domain score and each factor score is the mean of the answered items rescaled to a 0-100 scale, and required at least 75% of its items to be answered. All 115 participants met this rule. All partial Spearman correlations are adjusted for age and sex (female or not), with 95% confidence intervals from the Fisher z transformation (standard error 1/√(n − 3 − k), where k is the number of covariates).
